1. THEME: the whole conversation lives in the key's domain (the attribute description). 2. 3. HEDGE BEAT (session 0, the directed hedge user turn, after the prior-establish beat): the user voices a genuine UNREALIZED personal plan to SWITCH from the current value (prior_value) to VALUE, at exactly the directed strength — deliberating (thinking about / considering / can't decide), intending (might / probably / planning to / hoping to / leaning toward), or scheduled_but_future (committed but not yet done: about to / scheduled / once X happens I'll switch). 5. RESOLUTION BEAT (session <final>, user turn 0 — the FIRST user turn of the final session): a natural new-conversation opener in which the user reports what became of the earlier plan, as of a later day.    - open: the plan is STILL pending (still haven't decided, still weighing it, waiting on a sale); NO beat anywhere in the conversation resolves or abandons it; the follow-on beats continue the domain task while the choice stays open.
