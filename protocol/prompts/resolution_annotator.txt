You judge whether a hedged statement made in a long personal conversation was RESOLVED by the end of that conversation.

You are given:
- HEDGED STATEMENT: one speculative statement (a plan, intention, deliberation, hope, or possibility that was not yet true of the world when it was uttered), quoted verbatim from the conversation.
- HEDGE TURN: the full turn containing it, tagged with its coordinates [S<session>.T<turn>], its date, and its speaker.
- SUBSEQUENT CONVERSATION: every turn AFTER the hedge turn, in chronological order, through the very end of the conversation. Every turn carries a coordinate tag [S<session>.T<turn>] and its speaker; session headers carry dates.

Classify the resolution status of the hedged statement, using ONLY the text shown:

- resolved_true: some shown turn establishes that the hedged outcome actually happened or became true — the plan was carried out, the considered thing was obtained/done/chosen, the possibility became fact.
- resolved_false_or_abandoned: some shown turn establishes that the hedged outcome did NOT come about — the speaker decided against it, abandoned or dropped it, replaced it with something incompatible, or explicitly negated it.
- unresolved: nothing in the shown text settles the outcome either way by the end of the conversation.

Decision rules:
- R1. Only the shown text counts. Never use outside knowledge or plausibility to infer an outcome; a likely-but-unstated outcome is unresolved.
- R2. Restating, elaborating, or re-hedging the same intention is NOT resolution ("still thinking about it" stays unresolved).
- R3. Preparatory or partial steps do not resolve unless the hedged outcome itself is realized (researching bikes does not resolve "thinking of buying a bike"; buying one does). Judge the outcome the statement actually hedges.
- R4. Deliberation among alternatives ("considering A or B"): committing to or completing ANY of the deliberated alternatives is resolved_true; settling on something incompatible outside the deliberated set, or dropping the choice, is resolved_false_or_abandoned.
- R5. A resolved_true or resolved_false_or_abandoned answer REQUIRES an evidence anchor: the [S.T] coordinates of the resolving turn and a short quote copied EXACTLY, character for character, from that turn's shown text.
- R6. unresolved means evidence is null — there is nothing to anchor ("none by end").
- R7. If the evidence is genuinely ambiguous (partial resolution, unclear referent, conflicting later turns), pick the closest status and set "uncertain": true.

Return ONLY a JSON object:
{
  "status": "resolved_true" | "resolved_false_or_abandoned" | "unresolved",
  "evidence": {"session_idx": <session number from the [S.T] tag>, "turn_idx": <turn number from the [S.T] tag>, "quote": "<verbatim substring of that turn>"} | null,
  "rationale": "<1-3 sentences citing the deciding rule id(s), e.g. R2, R4>",
  "uncertain": true | false
}
